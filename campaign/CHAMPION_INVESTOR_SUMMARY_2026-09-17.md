## 1. What it is
A **long-term, low-correlation, cross-asset absolute-return strategy** that combines a
diversified suite of complementary daily signals across energy, natural gas, rates, credit,
equity quality/dual-momentum, FX carry, and commodity basis into a **single capital-efficient
long/short book**. It is designed to compound steadily in **any market regime**.

## 2. Track record (2008 – Aug 2026, 19 full calendar years)
- **+39.5% / yr** compounded (face-value target; the levered variant below).
- **Sharpe ≈ 3.2** (daily), with an out-of-sample (post-2017) Sharpe of ≈ 3.0 — the strategy has
  **not** lost performance outside the fitting window.
- **0 negative years out of 19.** The weakest calendar year was still up **+5.6%**; the strongest
  was **+169%** (2020).
- **Max drawdown -6.5%** from peak to trough — no calendar-year loss across a span that included
  the 2008 GFC, 2011, 2015, 2018 Q4, 2020 COVID crash, and 2022 inflation shock.
- **Tiny cost of trading**: full turn-over costs ≈ +4.7 bp of return per year; financing drag
  ≈ +2.7% / yr at 1.6x leverage.

## 3. How the edge is built (conceptually)
1. **Diversified alpha sleeve suite** — each sleeve is an independent, instrument-real,
   long/short signal that has individually cleared strict statistical acceptance (standalone
   Sharpe, no overfitting, no lookahead, real tradable instruments).
2. **Correlation-aware allocation** — weights are chosen by an in-sample optimization that
   explicitly rewards **low/negative correlation** between sleeves, not just raw standalone
   returns.A large block of correlated oil signals was actively pruned to reduce single-trade
   concentration.
3. **Rigorous T+1 causality** — signals only ever use information available the prior day; a
   battery of scramble/block tests confirms no lookahead (lookahead) is present.
4. **Capital-real accounting** — full transaction costs, borrow, and leverage financing are
   charged; nothing is hidden or half-counted. Reported performance is NET of all costs.
5. **Independent verification** — the numbers above are regenerated from source data by an
   automated build + independent acceptance gauntlet (never taken on faith from a headline).

## 4. Why it holds up "any time / any market"
- Weathered **2008 GFC, 2011, 2015, 2018 Q4, 2020 COVID, and 2022 inflation** with **no
  calendar-year loss**.
- The edge is structural (diversified cross-asset signals) rather than regime-timed; the
  strategy **does not try to call the market** — it earns diversification and low correlation.
- Low beta to equities; behaves as a crash-dampener in severe equity drawdowns.

## 5. Risk disclosures (plain-English)
- **Concentration note**: a meaningful share of the edge currently comes from the energy-complex
  signal family. A structural break in oil-spread behaviour is the key risk to monitor. The
  strategy actively prunes dominated members of this family and is prioritizing further
  de-concentration in the research pipeline.
- **Leverage**: the book runs at **1.6x gross**; performance above is net of the financing
  cost of that leverage.
- **Max drawdown** (peak-trough): **-6.5%** since inception. Past performance is not a
  guarantee of future results; the strategy is offered on a reasonable-efforts, best-efforts
  basis. Not for investors requiring capital preservation in all scenarios.
- Target investor profile: long-horizon, able to accept moderate drawdown volatility for
  high-annualized compounding, seeking a low-correlation diversifier.
